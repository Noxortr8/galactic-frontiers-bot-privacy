Galactic Frontiers Bot is designed to support the Star Wars: Galactic Frontiers Discord community through moderation, support tickets, verification, and a Star Wars-themed card game. We aim to handle information responsibly and only in connection with operating, maintaining, and improving these features.

This policy describes categories of information the bot may process. The exact information involved depends on which features are used, server configuration, and the systems operated by the bot's maintainers.
01 / Overview

Who this policy applies to

This policy applies when you interact with Galactic Frontiers Bot, including by using its commands, buttons, menus, moderation features, verification flows, support tickets, or card-game features in a Discord server.

Discord is a separate service. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own terms and privacy policy.

02 / Information we may process

Information connected to bot features

Depending on how you interact with the bot, information may include:

Discord account and server information

  • Discord user IDs, usernames, display names, and avatars;
  • server and channel IDs, server membership, roles, and permission-related information.

Moderation information

  • warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, reports, and staff notes;
  • relevant message content, message IDs, or other context needed to review a moderation action.

Support ticket information

  • messages, replies, attachments, and ticket transcripts;
  • ticket participants, relevant account IDs, and timestamps.

Verification information

  • verification status, completion timestamps, submitted responses, and access-related records.

Card-game information

  • card collections, inventory, card ownership, game progress, balances or points;
  • trades, match results, and gameplay statistics.

Technical and diagnostic information

  • command or interaction usage, interaction IDs, and timestamps;
  • error reports, diagnostic logs, and information needed to investigate failures or abuse.

Not every category is collected from every user or during every interaction.

03 / How information is used

Operating the community features

Information may be used to:

  • respond to commands and provide bot features;
  • moderate the server and help staff investigate reports or rule violations;
  • create, manage, and respond to support tickets;
  • verify members and manage access to server areas;
  • save card collections, trades, matches, and other game progress;
  • maintain reliability, troubleshoot errors, prevent abuse, and protect the bot and community;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations or respond to valid legal requests.
04 / Moderation & support

Staff tools and ticket handling

When moderation or support features are used, relevant information may be made available to authorized server moderators, support staff, or bot maintainers so they can review reports, respond to tickets, and carry out moderation actions.

Ticket content may include information you choose to submit. Please avoid sharing passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information unless it is genuinely necessary and you understand who will be able to view it.

05 / Verification & gameplay

Verification and the card game

Verification features may process your account ID, submitted responses, verification status, and timestamps to determine whether the required steps have been completed and to manage access.

Card-game features may process game-related records such as collections, inventory, points, trades, match outcomes, and statistics. These records allow the bot to maintain gameplay state and provide the game experience.

06 / Storage & security

How information is stored and protected

Information may be stored in databases, configuration files, application logs, backups, or other systems used to operate the bot. The exact storage method depends on the feature and the infrastructure in use.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. However, no online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Access to stored information should be limited to people who need it to maintain the bot, provide support, or administer the community.
07 / Sharing & third parties

When information may be disclosed

Information may be accessible to authorized server administrators and staff, the bot's maintainers, and infrastructure or service providers involved in hosting, storage, monitoring, or maintaining the bot, where relevant to their roles.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to respond to valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the community, or the service.

We do not intend to sell personal information. Discord and any third-party services have their own privacy practices and terms.

08 / Retention & deletion

How long information is kept

Information may be retained for as long as it is needed to provide bot features, maintain moderation and support records, preserve game progress, resolve disputes, maintain security, or meet legal obligations.

Different types of information may be retained for different periods. There is no single fixed retention period promised for every category. Backups and technical logs may persist for a period after information is changed or deleted from active systems.

Where practical and appropriate, information may be deleted, corrected, or anonymized when it is no longer needed.

09 / Your choices & rights

Questions or requests about your information

You may contact the community's staff through its official Discord server and support/ticket system to ask questions or submit a request relating to information associated with your use of the bot.

To help locate relevant records, include your Discord user ID and a clear description of your request. We may need to verify that a request relates to you before taking action. Some records may need to be retained for moderation, security, operational, or legal reasons.

10 / Children's privacy

Age and eligibility

The bot is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws or Discord's age requirements. If you believe a child has provided information inappropriately, contact the server staff through the official support system.

11 / Changes & contact

Updates to this policy

This policy may be updated as the bot's features, infrastructure, or practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Continued use of the bot after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Galactic Frontiers community staff through the official Discord server's support/ticket system.
